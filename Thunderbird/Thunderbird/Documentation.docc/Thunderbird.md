# ``Thunderbird``

Open Source Email App for iOS

## Overview

Thunderbird for iOS is a new development effort to create a high-quality email client tailored for iPhone and iPad. The app is in active development and available as a pre-release build through [TestFlight.](https://testflight.apple.com/join/ER3hRBSz)

What works today:

* Adding an account, with settings found automatically or entered by hand
* Signing in with a password
* Listing an account's mailboxes and the emails in them, and reading an email, over IMAP or JMAP
* Writing an email in the compose screen

In progress or still to come, among other things: signing in to IMAP with OAuth ([#535](https://github.com/thunderbird/thunderbird-ios/issues/535)), sending from the app, storing mail for offline use, search and notifications.
